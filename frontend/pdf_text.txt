FEASIBILITY & VIABILITYTeam: DEADLOCK
Feasibility
PAIMANA already has regularly updated project information, making the idea practical without replacing the 
existing monitoring system. The proposed solution can act as an additional intelligence layer to help officials 
identify projects that may require early attention.
Challenges & Risks
Incomplete or inconsistent data, differences 
across project sectors, missing real-world risk 
factors, false warnings, and lack of trust in 
automated predictions.
Mitigation
Data-quality checks, comparison with similar 
projects, clear explanations for every warning, 
gradual improvement using additional data, and 
keeping final decisions with human officials.
