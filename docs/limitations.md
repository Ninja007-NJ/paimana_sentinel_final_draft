# Limitations

- Predictions depend on PAIMANA reporting quality and update timeliness.
- Estimates are statistical associations, not causal findings or guaranteed outcomes.
- What-if outputs describe model behavior, not intervention impact.
- OCMS-to-PAIMANA reporting scope and semantics changed.
- Generalization to unseen projects is weaker than repeat monitoring of known projects.
- Calibration remains imperfect; Model V1.1 status is YELLOW.
- Peer groups can reflect source metadata inconsistencies and always require human review.
- Six-month delay and cost escalation are not production models; cost targets are especially sparse.
- External operational variables absent from public monitoring data may materially affect real project outcomes.
